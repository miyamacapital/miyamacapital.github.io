PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains how Miyama Capital LLC collects, uses, and protects the personal data you provide through this website, in particular for research report subscriptions.

← Back to Home •

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1) Scope

This policy applies to www.miyamacap.com (including the /zh/, /en/, and /jp/ language versions) and to the data you provide when subscribing to research reports through this website. The data controller is Miyama Capital LLC, a California limited liability company.

2) Data We Collect

  • Subscription form: name and email address; organization/title is optional. We also record the page language in which you filled out the form, the referring page, the time of submission, and the times at which you clicked the confirmation and unsubscribe links.
  • Sending records: each report sent to you leaves a record of the sending time and result, so that we do not send duplicates.
  • Website analytics: this website uses Google Analytics, which collects anonymized browsing statistics through cookies (such as pages visited, device type, and approximate region). You can opt out through your browser settings or Google's opt-out tool.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as national ID numbers or financial account details, and we do not pass your personal data in URL parameters.

3) Purpose and Legal Basis

With your consent, we use the data above to:

  • send Miyama Capital research reports (PDF) and related notices;
  • send necessary service messages, such as subscription confirmation and unsubscribe confirmation emails;
  • decide what to send based on your language preference;
  • keep the list accurate and prevent abuse (for example, blocking repeated bot submissions).

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Research reports are general analysis and do not constitute investment advice.

4) Data Storage and Processors

Your data is stored in Miyama Capital's Google Workspace account (Google LLC, servers located in the United States), including Google Sheets (the subscriber list), Gmail (sending), and Google Drive (report files). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). This means your data is transferred outside Taiwan and is protected by these providers under their own security standards.

Access to the list is limited to the Miyama Capital staff responsible for sending research.

5) Retention

  • Confirmed subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe.
  • After you unsubscribe: we keep your email address and the unsubscribe record to make sure we do not email you again; the remaining fields are deleted within a reasonable period.
  • Unconfirmed requests: receive no reports, and the related data is purged periodically.
  • You can also email us at any time to request deletion of all your data (see Section 7).

6) Unsubscribing

Every email includes a personal unsubscribe link at the bottom. Clicking it takes effect immediately, with no login or reply required. You can also email connect@miyamacap.com to request removal.

7) Your Rights

Under the Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act, Article 3, and other applicable laws, you may request, with respect to your personal data:

  • access or review;
  • a copy;
  • supplementation or correction;
  • cessation of collection, processing, or use;
  • deletion.

Please email connect@miyamacap.com; we will handle your request within a reasonable period after verifying your identity. If you do not provide your name and email address, we cannot complete your subscription, but this does not affect your ability to browse this website.

8) Minors

The research on this website is intended for adult readers. If we learn that a person under 18 has provided data without the consent of a legal guardian, we will delete it.

9) Changes to This Policy

If this policy is modified, we will update the date on this page and publish the new version. For material changes, we will notify you at the email address you subscribed with.
