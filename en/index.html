Philosophy

The Miyama Philosophy

Compounding, Rooted in Discipline.

Regime-aware research, disciplined allocation, institutional risk controls.

  1. 01

    Macro Regime Adaptive

    "Markets shift. Portfolios adapt."

    A flexible multi-asset framework across equities, rates, FX, and volatility—designed to navigate shifting macro regimes without anchoring to a single narrative.

  2. 02

    Asymmetric Risk Control

    "Survival precedes profit."

    Hard drawdown limits, liquidity awareness, and predefined exits. Stop-losses are treated as the premium paid to protect long-term compounding.

  3. 03

    Quantitative Discipline

    "Precision is a habit, not a stroke of luck."

    Quantitative tools and AI are extensions of discipline—systematically executing transmission analysis and reducing human bias. Machine models classify macro regimes and estimate probability shifts; the founder's judgment translates these into cross-asset positioning.

FOUNDER'S VISION

In a world of noise, clarity comes from discipline.

"In a world of noise, clarity comes from discipline. We don't strive to be right every time; we strive to survive every scenario and thrive in the trends that matter."

The founder's cross-market judgment — reading how policy shifts, geopolitical events, and structural forces transmit across currencies, rates, equities, and real assets — is the core source of alpha.

In noisy markets, discipline is the only controllable edge. We do not aim to be correct on every position; instead, we use rigorous risk management and dynamic regime awareness to protect capital during systemic stress and deploy risk into high-conviction macro trends when probabilities are skewed in our favor.
